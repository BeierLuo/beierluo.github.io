research

My research focuses on building reliable and trustworthy large language models. I am broadly interested in understanding, modeling, and improving the confidence and uncertainty behavior of modern machine learning systems, particularly under distribution shift and open-ended generation. Current directions:

  • confidence calibration and uncertainty estimation for LLMs
  • detection and mitigation of hallucinations in LLMs
  • theoretical and empirical foundations of reliable machine learning
